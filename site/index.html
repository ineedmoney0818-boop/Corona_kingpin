Questions

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The free plan is the trial.

What's a credit?

One unit of AI work. Every button shows how many it uses before you tap it.

What if a render fails?

Your credits come back automatically, and you'll see a note saying so.

Do unused credits expire?

They roll over for one month.

How do I cancel?

Settings, Plan, Cancel. Or use the link in any renewal email. You keep everything until the period ends.

Will you remind me before you charge me?

Yes. 3 days before a monthly renewal, 7 days before an annual one.

Can I bring videos from other apps?

Yes. Upload any MP4 or MOV, or photos.
