## 2. The problem
- **Most AI video apps let you do the work, then ask for money.** You edit for an hour and the export button turns into a paywall.
- **Credits disappear on renders that fail.** The app breaks, the credits are gone, and you start over.

## 3. How it works
1. **Drop in** a video, or a few photos.
2. **Reelplain drafts** clips or a carousel with captions. Each step shows its credit cost before it runs.
3. **Change anything** on your phone (trim, caption text, caption position), then export or post.

## 4. Features (fixes first)
- **The price is on the button.** You see what an action costs before you tap it.
- **Failed renders are free.** If it breaks, your credits come back automatically.
- **Export is never a trap.** On the free plan, everything you make can be exported, with a small watermark.
- **Cancel in one tap.** You keep your plan and credits until the period ends.
- **Full editing on your phone.** The AI's draft is a starting point, not a final answer.
- **Your footage stays yours.** No AI-regenerated images unless you ask for them. Auto-reframe is off until you turn it on.
- **Caption presets you keep.** Save your favourite style, move captions, or turn them off.
- *Parity (not headlined):* AI clip finding, auto captions, reels and carousels, 9:16/1:1/4:5 formats.

## 5. Pricing
The table from `pricing.md`: Try $0 · Creator $19 · Studio $49, with 2 months free on annual plans.

## 6. FAQ
- **Do I need a card to try it?** No. The free plan is the trial.
- **What's a credit?** One unit of AI work. Every button shows how many it uses before you tap it.
- **What happens if a render fails?** You get the credits back automatically. You'll see a note saying so.
- **Do unused credits expire?** They roll over for one month.
- **How do I cancel?** Settings → Plan → Cancel, or from the link in any renewal email. You keep everything until the period ends.
- **Will you remind me before you charge me?** Yes: 3 days before a monthly renewal, 7 days before an annual one.
- **Can I bring videos from other apps?** Yes. Upload any MP4 or MOV, or photos. (There's no project importer from other tools, so we don't claim one.)
- **Do you train AI on my videos?** Answer truthfully once the AI vendor and its data terms are chosen. **Do not publish a claim before it's true.**
